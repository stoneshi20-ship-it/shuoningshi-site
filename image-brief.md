# Labyrinth — Product Spec + AI Image Script

Everything an AI (or a factory) needs: dimensions, groove geometry, material, light behaviour,
and the base image set (colors, backgrounds, sizes) with ready-to-paste prompts.

---

## 1 · DIMENSIONS (mm)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Overall form | **Saucer / biconvex lens** — a gently domed top, a rounded underside, thickest at the center, tapering to a **soft rounded equator ridge** (UFO / smooth pebble) |
| Overall diameter | **Ø140** |
| Overall height | **24** (dome top ~15 + rounded base ~9, measured at center) |
| Edge | the widest point is a **rounded equator (full bull-nose)** — no chamfer line, no hard corner |
| Base | gently convex, a **recessed circular power/charge contact** at the bottom apex; soft non-slip ring |
| Weight | ≈ **590 g** (hollowed base; feels substantial, "worth picking up") |
| Center dish | a **fully spherical shallow dish, Ø20 × 3 deep** at the domed center — the groove flows tangentially into it |

## 2 · GROOVE GEOMETRY (the entire interface)

- **Type:** a single **unicursal spiral** — one continuous path, **no branches, no dead ends**. Finger always reaches the center.
- **Turns:** **2 turns**, outer entry → center dish.
- **Pitch** (gap between adjacent grooves): **20 mm** center-to-center.
- **Land** (flat ridge between grooves): **6 mm** wide, softly rounded (no sharp ridge).
- **Cross-section:** a smooth **U-channel sized to a fingertip** — **width 14 mm**, **depth 5 mm**.
  - **Bottom radius R5** (cradles the finger pad).
  - **Top edges rounded R2** (no lip — the finger slides in and out smoothly).
- **Entry:** starts at **R57** from center (≈6 mm clear of the rounded rim). The mouth has a **funnel + depth run-out** — the groove **fades from 0 depth at the rim to full D5** over the first stretch, a vanishing lead-in so the finger glides in from the flat top.
- **Travel length:** one-way ≈ **470 mm** → paced over the breathing cycle, ~2–3 min per trip.
- **Finish inside groove:** slightly **satin/matte** so the finger glides with a little friction (not slippery, not sticky).

> **Edge rule (non-negotiable):** *no sharp corners or hard edges anywhere on the product.* The outer profile is a **full bull-nose (R6)**; every rim, groove lip, land and the center dish is radiused and blended — it must feel entirely rounded and soft in the hand.

## 2b · POWER & CONTROLS (no visible buttons)

- **Lift-to-wake / set-down-to-sleep** — a presence/tilt sensor in the base wakes the light when picked up or touched, sleeps it when set down. The top face stays perfectly clean — no button breaks the form.
- **On/off + charge** — a single **recessed circular contact on the base**: long-press to fully power on/off; **USB-C (or wireless) charging** through the same area.
- Rationale: any physical switch would add a hard edge / seam and clutter the calm object — so the only "control" is picking it up.

> **Edge rule (non-negotiable):** *no sharp corners or hard edges anywhere on the product.* Every edge, rim, groove lip, land and the center dish is radiused and blended — it must feel entirely rounded and soft in the hand.

## 3 · MATERIAL & FINISH

- **Base model:** **white ceramic** (matte satin glaze), warm-white / bone tone — calm, tactile, premium, "object not gadget."
- **Premium variants:** dark **graphite anodized aluminium**; pale **natural stone** (e.g. honed marble).
- **Surface:** satin/matte everywhere (no gloss glare); groove interior very slightly smoother than the top land.
- **Base underside:** soft silicone foot ring, same tone, matte.

## 4 · LIGHT (how the glow works)

- A **ring of tiny LEDs sits under the top plate**, feeding a **thin light-guide / diffuser strip that runs along the bottom of the groove**. So the light appears to live *inside the channel*, as a soft continuous line — never visible bulbs.
- **Behaviour:** a single **soft point of light travels along the groove** as the breathing pacer:
  - moves **inward while you breathe in** (~5 s), **pauses at center**, moves **outward while you breathe out** (~7 s).
  - The rest of the groove holds a **very faint ambient glow**; the moving point is brighter.
  - When your finger **keeps pace with the light**, the traced section **warms up** (cool→warm white) as positive feedback.
- **Color:** primary glow **soft cyan-white (#8FD6FF → #EAF8FF core)**; in-sync feedback shifts to **warm amber (#FFB26B)**. Low brightness, diffuse, calm — think "breathing," not "notification."
- **Off state:** no light; it reads as a plain white ceramic disc with an engraved spiral (still beautiful).

## 5 · BASE IMAGE SET — colors · backgrounds · sizes

Consistent recipe so the whole set looks like one product.

- **Product color (base set):** matte **white ceramic**, warm-white / bone.
- **Light in renders:** soft **cyan-white** glow inside the groove (subtle, low intensity).
- **Background:** **light neutral** — warm off-white to pale grey seamless (#F2F1EE → #E7E8EA). Soft studio light, gentle single shadow. (Keeps it calm and prints/exports cleanly.)
- **Mood:** minimal, serene, high-end, lots of negative space.
- **Sizes:** hero/landscape **1536×1024**, square **1024×1024**, portrait **1024×1536**.

> like a smooth pebble / UFO)** — no sharp corners anywhere. A **single continuous spiral groove** is
> engraved into the gently domed top face (2 turns, a smooth U-shaped channel ~14 mm wide and 5 mm deep
> sized to a fingertip, its outer entry fading in from the surface), flowing into a **small spherical dish
> at the very center**. A **thin soft cyan-white light glows gently from inside the groove**. Warm off-white
> seamless background, soft studio lighting, one gentle shadow, calm and serene, lots of negative space,
> ultra-detailed, photorealistic, 8k. No text, no logos, no visible buttons or LEDs.

### TIPS
- Keep the STYLE ANCHOR identical every time. Generate **hero first**, pick the best, then use it as an **image reference** (`--sref` / reference image) for all others so ceramic tone, glow and proportions stay consistent.
- If a tool over-brightens the light, add: "very subtle low-intensity glow, mostly-off."
- For Midjourney add aspect flags: `--ar 3:2` / `1:1` / `2:3`.
